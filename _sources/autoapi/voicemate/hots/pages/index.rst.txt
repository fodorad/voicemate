voicemate.hots.pages
====================

.. py:module:: voicemate.hots.pages

.. autoapi-nested-parse::

   Parsers for the saved Icy Veins pages (plain dataclasses out, no knowledge-base logic).

   The pages are saved by Adam from his browser into the pages folder. Each parser reads one kind
   of page: a hero guide, a tier list, or a text page (map guides, abilities, talents).

.. py:class:: HeroGuide

   What a hero's build-guide page says about the draft.

   :ivar slug: Hero slug.
   :ivar name: Display name.
   :ivar role: Role from the page breadcrumb (singular), e.g. ``Melee Assassin``.
   :ivar modified: The page's ``dateModified`` (ISO 8601), empty when absent.
   :ivar strengths: Strength bullet points.
   :ivar weaknesses: Weakness bullet points.
   :ivar synergies: Slugs of heroes that work well with this one.
   :ivar counters: Slugs of heroes that counter this one.
   :ivar maps: Map slug → ``stronger`` | ``average`` | ``weaker``.
   :ivar synergy_text: The page's explanation of the synergies.
   :ivar counter_text: The page's explanation of the counters.
