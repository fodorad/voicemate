voicemate.hots.screen.interpreter
=================================

.. py:module:: voicemate.hots.screen.interpreter

.. autoapi-nested-parse::

   What a draft screen says: phase, map, timer and the heroes on each team.

   A pure function over a :class:`~voicemate.hots.screen.reader.Frame`: it knows where the game
   puts things (hero names in big capitals at the left edge for Adam's team and the right edge for
   the enemy, the map name at the top, the phase in the middle) and nothing about how the text was
   recognized. Player names are ignored.

.. py:class:: DraftScreen

   The draft as one screen shows it.

   :ivar phase: The current phase.
   :ivar map_slug: The map, when its name was read.
   :ivar seconds: The countdown, when it was read.
   :ivar ally: Heroes locked in on Adam's team, top slot first.
   :ivar enemy: Heroes locked in on the enemy team, top slot first.
   :ivar pending: Heroes shown but not locked in yet (hovered picks of either team).
   :ivar banned: Heroes named by a "BANNED" announcement.
   :ivar invalid: Heroes shown on Adam's side that the enemy has or that are banned.
