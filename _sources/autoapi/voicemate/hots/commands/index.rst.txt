.. py:class:: Command

   A recognized screen or mode command.

   :ivar kind: ``"read"`` (look at the screen), ``"last"`` (read the newest saved screenshot),
               ``"brief"`` (what the two teams mean for the match), ``"on"`` or ``"off"`` (draft
               mode).
   :ivar advice: What to advise after reading: ``"ban"``, ``"pick"`` or ``""`` (what fits the
                 phase).



   .. py:attribute:: kind
      :type:  str


   .. py:attribute:: advice
      :type:  str
      :value: ''



.. py:function:: parse(said: str) -> Command | None

   The command in ``said`` (already cleaned, see ``intents.clean``), or ``None``.
