voicemate.report
================

.. py:module:: voicemate.report

.. autoapi-nested-parse::

   Summaries of the per-turn metrics log (``make report``).

   This is the Stage-6 feedback loop: when a budget from docs/sdlc/intent.md is breached, the
   report says so, and the finding goes back into the intent as new work.

.. py:class:: Report

   Aggregated turn metrics.

   :ivar turns: Number of turns.
   :ivar chat_first_audio: p50/p95 first-audio latency of turns without tools.
   :ivar tool_first_audio: p50/p95 first-audio latency of turns with tools.
   :ivar error_rate: Share of failed turns.
   :ivar tool_counts: Calls per tool.
   :ivar breaches: Human-readable budget violations.

      :type:  int
      :value: 0
