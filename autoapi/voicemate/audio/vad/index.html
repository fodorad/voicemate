voicemate.audio.vad¶

Voice activity detection and utterance segmentation (endpointing).

Segmenter turns a continuous 16 kHz microphone stream into utterances. It is independent of the speech-probability model: SileroVAD is used at runtime and the deterministic EnergyVAD is handy for tests and as a dependency-free fallback.
