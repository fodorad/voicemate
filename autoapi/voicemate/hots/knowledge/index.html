voicemate.hots.knowledge¶

The local HotS knowledge base, filled from the saved Icy Veins pages.

Knowledge keeps one SQLite file. Knowledge.refresh() compares the pages folder with what was imported last time (file size and modification time) and re-imports only the pages that are new or changed, so starting Ava costs almost nothing once the pages are known. Ava never fetches the pages: Adam saves them from his browser, and a changed page shows up in the RefreshReport so he knows what he re-saved.

class voicemate.hots.knowledge.RefreshReport[source]¶

What Knowledge.refresh() did.

Variables:

  • added – Pages imported for the first time.

  • updated – Pages whose file changed and were imported again.

  • removed – Pages no longer in the folder (their rows were dropped).

  • failed – (page, reason) for pages that could not be parsed (old rows are kept).

  • unchanged – Number of pages left alone.
