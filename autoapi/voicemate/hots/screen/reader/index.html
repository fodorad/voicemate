voicemate.hots.screen.reader¶

What a screen read returns, and the seam every screen source implements.

A ScreenReader turns “look at the screen” into a Frame: the picture (for colours and portraits) and the text recognized on it. Positions are fractions of the picture (0-1, measured from the top-left), so nothing downstream depends on the resolution.
