app¶

NiceGUI front end: one page per conversation plus the /ws/audio WebSocket.

The page renders the pipeline rail (one station per Lane), the conversation, an activity trace that marks every request that left the machine, the notes panel and, when a tool needs permission, a confirmation dialog. Microphone and speaker audio travel over a plain WebSocket so the binary stream stays out of NiceGUI’s own socket.
